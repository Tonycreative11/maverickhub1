01 — Note

Sell the morning, not the motor

Appliance films usually open on the product with a specification super. This one opens on a hand, a cup and steam, and lets the machine arrive as the reason the shot exists.

The supers stay conversational — “the ideal home”, “simply ideal” — because the film is a mood piece with a product in it, not a leaflet.

01 — Brief

Two films, one campaign

The launch needed a film and something that could survive a feed. Instead of cropping one edit down, the campaign was delivered as two finished vertical films: a narrative spot that stays at the table, and a faster piece that carries the app through the same world.

Both were finished in Spanish, with the interface language matching the market rather than being redrawn for it.

02 — Direction

The lamp is the antagonist

One practical overhead light does all the work. It picks out hands, chips and faces and leaves everything else black, which meant the camera could stay low and still and let the performances carry the frame.

The app appears only when the hand turns — the phone is a consequence of the scene, never the reason for it.

The table is the set. Everything else is a reaction shot.

03 — Cut

Two tempos, one board

The table film is patient: wide overhead, then faces, then chips. Sound carries the tension — chips, breath, the lamp — and the score only arrives once the outcome is already decided.

The second film is cut to the length of a scroll: arcade neon, the app opening in the dark, the same player moving through it. Same grade, same type, different pulse.

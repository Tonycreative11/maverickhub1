01 — Note

One room, two voices

Written as a single-location piece so that all of the production value could go into the face and the light rather than into coverage. The horror is contractual rather than violent: something is offering a deal, and the deal is legible.

The film leans on the pause before an answer — the cut holds longer than is comfortable, which is where the tension actually lives.

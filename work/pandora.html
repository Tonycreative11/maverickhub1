01 — Note

Hands, not faces

The cast is directed to use their hands the way people actually do — adjusting a cuff, holding a bag, touching a collar — so the jewellery is always in motion and never presented on a velvet tray.

Two looks, one light direction, cut to a single piece of music. The vertical frame keeps the wrist and the face in the same shot, which is the only composition that survives a feed.
